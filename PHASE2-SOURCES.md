- Hibiscus (karkade) and baobab have no line of their own in the table. They fall under "Other agricultural products" (73,208 t, $31.2 m).

- Customs prices for Sudan are thought to be **understated** because of rules on bringing export earnings back into the country. Treat the averages per tonne as a minimum, not a market price.

## 3. Combined with the user's data pack (sesame)

- Production: **626,173 t in 2024** (FAOSTAT, flag X). Exports in 2025: **288,941 t** (CBoS). Exports were about 46% of the previous year's harvest (calculated).
- 2026 risk: rainfall **30% below average in Gedaref and 20% in Sennar**. These two states grew about half of 2023/24 sesame (CFSAM Table 12; FAO GIEWS, Sept 2026).
- Sudanese pound: about **7,500 SDG/USD** on the unofficial market (Sept 2026, WFP). Grain prices doubled in 2026, a cost risk for buying and transport inside Sudan.

- Every supplier shows the same price per kg ($2.41 for sesame, $4.42 for gum), so WITS has probably **estimated the quantities from the values**. Trust the dollar values more than the tonnes.
- **Gum arabic:** Saudi Arabia is a very small market, so a Saudi gum factory would have to export. Added to the site as a risk and in Compare.
- **Sesame:** Sudan is already Saudi Arabia's main supplier. Added to the site as a strength.

**Powder margin (calculated):** selling price $4,500–7,000/t − raw cost (raw price ÷ 0.79 yield) − about $500/t processing (an estimate):
- raw $2,500/t → +$835 to +$3,335 per tonne of powder
- raw $3,200/t → −$51 to +$2,449
- raw $5,000/t → −$2,329 to +$171

The site now uses **$300–1,500/t** as a middle case (it was $1,000–2,000) and explains the spread.

**Granules, acacia fibre and medicine grade, Talha:** no bulk price data found. Laboratory catalogue prices ($40–100/kg for 100–500 g) say nothing about bulk prices. These routes are now marked **Estimate**, and Compare's quick answers **leave out unchecked estimates** for any crop that has at least one checked line.
